# Product name

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Map: [Reelio MVP](../map.md)

## Question

What is the product called, now that "Reelio" has failed clearance ([Reelio brand clearance](./15-brand-clearance.md))?

- Shortlist candidates and pick one with the human.
- Clear the pick before committing to it:
    - **Trademark registers:** the USPTO (exact and similar marks, Classes 9, 41, 42), EUIPO via TMview, and the UK IPO.
    - **App stores:** the App Store, Google Play and Product Hunt, especially movie, TV and watchlist apps.
    - **Domains:** `.com` and fallbacks such as `.app`, `.film`, `.movie`. Check which matter given US-first.
- Confirm the name works with the TMDB attribution rule that Reelio's mark must be more prominent than the TMDB logo, and that it doesn't suggest endorsement by TMDB, IMDb or JustWatch.

This blocks launch, not the spec: the spec keeps "Reelio" as the working name, and the rename becomes a find-and-replace ticket in implementation.

## Comments

- Clearance bar agreed (2026-11-04):
    - **Form:** a single invented or evocative word, roughly 5 to 10 letters, easy to say and spell. No "reel" (crowded: Reelgood, Reelio, ReelStack, ReelGuide…), no generic descriptors, and no TMDB, IMDb, JustWatch, Cinema, Film or Movie as a hard part. The tone is a calm, personal "what do I watch tonight?" tool.
    - **Domains don't matter** (owner's call).
    - **Cleared means, from free sources only (not a legal opinion):** no identical or confusingly similar live mark in Classes 9, 41 or 42 (USPTO, EUIPO via TMview, UK IPO); no same-name app in the App Store or Google Play in entertainment, movies or lifestyle; no same-name product among the top web results. Any hit in a movie or watchlist product fails; hits in unrelated fields are noted and accepted.

## Answer

**The product is called Houselight** (chosen by the owner, 2026-11-04). The name refers to the theatre house lights dimming as the film begins, the moment you've picked tonight's film. Across three screening rounds it fits the "dimming the lights" theme the owner asked for and had the cleanest result: no app or near mark found in the field.

- **Not yet cleared on the official registers.** The screen used web search and aggregators. The remaining checks are a separate task, [Clear Houselight on the official registers](./21-houselight-register-clearance.md). If any of them fails, the next pick is Lightsdown (with care over the LIGHTS OUT marks) or a fresh round on the same theme.
- **Rejected:** Reelio (an existing TMDB watchlist app), SceneIt (the "Scene It?" movie trivia brand and its marks), plus every Fail or Risky entry in the screening tables above.
- **Domains don't matter** for the prototype: it runs on a `laravel.cloud` address.
- **The rename is an implementation ticket**, a find-and-replace once clearance passes: `config('app.name')`, the wordmark component, page titles and metadata, mail sender name, the TMDB, IMDb and JustWatch credit copy, and the docs (`GLOSSARY.md`, the spec). The wordmark must stay more prominent than the TMDB logo.
