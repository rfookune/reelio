## Question

Is "Houselight" clear on the official trademark registers and app stores, so the name chosen in [Product name](./20-product-name.md) can be adopted?

This is HITL: the registers need interactive searches. The owner runs:

1. **USPTO trademark search** (<https://tmsearch.uspto.gov>): `HOUSELIGHT`, `HOUSE LIGHT`, `HOUSELIGHTS`. Check live marks in Classes 9, 41 and 42. Open the HOUSELIGHTS LLC registration and note what goods it covers.
2. **TMview** (<https://www.tmdn.org/tmview>, which covers EUIPO and national EU offices) and the **UK IPO** (<https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk>): the same terms and classes.
3. **App Store and Google Play:** search "Houselight" and look for anything in entertainment, movies or lifestyle.

Record each result here with the date. It passes under the bar in [Product name](./20-product-name.md): no identical or confusingly similar live mark in Classes 9, 41 or 42, and no same-name app in the field. A hit in an unrelated field is noted and accepted.

It blocks launch, not the spec. If it fails, [Product name](./20-product-name.md) records the fallback.
