# Reelio MVP

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

A locked, implementation-ready spec for the Reelio MVP: every open decision settled, the domain model named in `GLOSSARY.md`, and the module and data design set out, ready for `/to-spec` → `/to-tickets`. P1/P2 features are listed in the spec as follow-ups, not designed.

## Notes

- **Source PRD:** [prd.md](./prd.md) (v1.0, 2026-11-04). It is the guideline, not gospel: decisions recorded on this map override it.
- **Scope:** PRD P0 plus its §17 "Recommended Initial Decisions" (auth required to save; TMDB as Catalog source; US-first, configurable country; picker limited to unwatched watchlist; personal ratings deferred; "Reelio" is a working name, to become "Houselight" ([Product name](./issues/20-product-name.md)); free, no billing). P1/P2 features are recorded as spec follow-ups only.
- **Scope change from PRD:** the **IMDb rating** (from FR-023, P2 in the PRD) is **in the MVP**, sourced from IMDb's official non-commercial datasets and shown alongside the TMDB rating. Rotten Tomatoes is a spec follow-up; Metacritic is out of scope; OMDb is not used. See [OMDb ratings: coverage, terms and limits](./issues/03-omdb-ratings.md).
- **Audience:** a real public beta, so licensing, attribution, brand, and production concerns are on the map.
- **Permanently non-commercial (standing, 2026-11-04):** Reelio will never earn money: no ads, affiliate links, paid tier, or sponsorships. TMDB's and IMDb's non-commercial licences depend on this; any change means re-opening both.
- **Stack baseline:** the existing starter kit (Laravel 13, Inertia v3, Vue 3, Fortify, reka-ui components, Wayfinder routes, Pest 5). Any departure from the PRD's §7.1 table is an explicit decision on this map, not a default. The starter kit's optional 2FA and passkeys stay.
- **Vocabulary:** use `GLOSSARY.md` terms (Catalog source, Rating source, External rating, Streaming service, Availability). Never say "provider" except inside TMDB-specific code.
- **Skills to consult:** `grilling` + `domain-modeling` for every grilling ticket; `codebase-design` for boundary and module tickets; `prototype` + `frontend-design` for UI tickets; `laravel-best-practices`, `inertia-vue-development`, `testing-best-practices` where the ticket touches those areas.
- **TMDB licence constraints (standing):** consistent with being non-commercial; no AI/ML on TMDB data, which constrains the P2/v2 follow-ups; TMDB-derived data must stay separable from user data and be purgeable. See [TMDB terms, attribution and caching limits](./issues/01-tmdb-terms.md).
- **Plan, don't do:** tickets produce decisions, not application code. Prototypes are throwaway.

## Decisions so far

<!-- one line per closed ticket: [title](link): gist -->

- [TMDB terms, attribution and caching limits](./issues/01-tmdb-terms.md): fine for a free beta under TMDB's non-commercial licence. Requires a TMDB logo and notice in the footer and an About page, a JustWatch text credit on every Availability block, refresh or purge within 6 months, hotlinked images only, no AI/ML on TMDB data, and no deep links to Streaming services (only TMDB's watch page). Any revenue needs a paid licence; SEO/"destination site" use is a grey area to confirm in writing.
- [TMDB API coverage for MVP features](./issues/02-tmdb-api-coverage.md): TMDB v3 covers every P0 feature. Homepage from trending, popular, top_rated, upcoming and discover by genre; search via `/search/movie`; the detail page in one `/movie/{id}` call with credits, videos, release_dates and watch/providers appended. Director = crew with job Director; the trailer must be ranked by Reelio. Availability for all countries comes in one response (flatrate/rent/buy plus free/ads) with one TMDB watch-page link per country. Pages capped at 500; trending can't be scoped to a country; ratings are TMDB's own only, with `imdb_id` as the OMDb join key; about 40 req/s, no SLA.
- [OMDb ratings: coverage, terms and limits](./issues/03-omdb-ratings.md): TMDB has no IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes or Metacritic ratings (`imdb_id` is the join key). OMDb fails the PRD's authorization test: its terms allow personal use only, it relays the scores without a licence, and new releases are often `N/A`. The licensed routes are IMDb via AWS Data Exchange and Rotten Tomatoes/Metacritic via Fandango or Fabric Origin, all priced by quote. **Decided:** Reelio is permanently non-commercial; the IMDb rating comes from IMDb's official non-commercial datasets (written confirmation from IMDb is a launch blocker); Rotten Tomatoes is a spec follow-up; Metacritic is out of scope; OMDb is not used.
- [The user–movie lifecycle](./issues/04-user-movie-lifecycle.md): Watchlist and Watched are independent private facts (four states). Marking as watched sets the Watched date to now and takes the Movie off the Watchlist (rewatch moves the date); remove and clear touch only their own fact; Undo restores the exact earlier state; account deletion hard-deletes it all. Terms: Watchlist, Watched, Watched date.
- [Infrastructure baseline](./issues/05-infrastructure-baseline.md): Laravel Cloud; Postgres everywhere (tests included); Redis cache; database queue and sessions; Cloud-managed workers and scheduler, no Horizon; hosted mail API; local plus production with preview environments, no staging; Herd locally; no object storage.
- [Visual language and app shell](./issues/06-visual-language-and-shell.md): charcoal dark-first palette with an electric-blue accent and a user theme toggle (defaulting to Dark, starter-kit Appearance settings kept), Inter, and shimmer skeletons. A top-bar shell with a pill nav and a mobile bottom nav (no sidebar). Discover has a backdrop hero over list rows; Watchlist and Watched use a poster card with a floating bookmark toggle. The starter kit's sidebar, breadcrumbs, dashboard and welcome page go.
- [Test strategy and tooling](./issues/07-test-strategy.md): Pest feature tests on Postgres carry the bulk; Vitest (`vp test`) only for client-side logic; Pest's browser plugin for five core journeys at desktop and mobile sizes. Sources are faked with in-memory fakes of Reelio's own interfaces, the TMDB adapter with recorded HTTP fixtures, the IMDb import with a tiny TSV; stray HTTP is blocked. A manual-only `live` group catches drift. CI: `ci:check` plus `vp test` and a browser job, both required on `main`; no coverage threshold.
- [Analytics and error monitoring](./issues/08-analytics-and-monitoring.md): first-party, server-side events in Postgres (signed-in `user_id` only, no cookies or banner, text of searches not stored), queued fail-soft, cascade-deleted with the account and rolled up after 13 months. Event list settled (`movie_rated` dropped; `availability_link_clicked` replaces the provider click). Nightwatch for the backend, Sentry (errors only, EU, no replay or IP) for the frontend; email alerts to the owner; success metrics via a hand-run `metrics:report` command, no dashboard.
- [Movie identity, URLs and local storage](./issues/09-movie-identity-and-storage.md): local-id URLs (`/movies/{slug}-{id}`) via a rate-limited entry route that creates the Movie row on first view; metadata in Postgres, detail-only data in cache; IMDb ratings in their own dataset table read via `imdb_id`; Availability stored per supported country for P1 filtering; `user_movies` with `watchlisted_at`/`watched_at` and restrictive movie FK; dropped Movies become shells, unviewed orphan rows are purged.
- [Catalog and Rating source boundary](./issues/10-source-boundary.md): separate Catalog source and Rating source seams in `App\Catalog` and `App\Ratings`. A neutral TMDB adapter sits behind one deep `Catalog` module (cache, rows, refresh, clamping, explicit `fresh`/`stale`/`unavailable` results). Ratings come from a batch `ratingsFor` where missing means absent. The IMDb import validates into a staging table and swaps. Timeouts of 3 s interactive and 10 s background, a circuit breaker, stale-on-error, and one cache-key builder.
- [Country and Availability presentation](./issues/11-country-and-availability.md): country detected from the edge header (no geo-IP, nothing stored), then overridden by an explicit choice (cookie or `preferred_country`), US fallback, and always named on the block. Ten supported countries, config-driven. Groups Stream, Free, Rent, Buy; entries not clickable, one labelled TMDB watch-page link; JustWatch credit, "not a guarantee" caveat and freshness line on every block. Trending stays global.
- [Movie picker](./issues/12-movie-picker.md): candidates are the whole Watchlist minus shells. One optional genre multi-select listing only genres present. Uniform server-side pick via `useHttp`; Pick again excludes only the previous pick. A dialog opened from "Pick for me" on the Watchlist page (hidden when it's empty), showing essential details, External ratings and stored Stream/Free Availability, with View details and Pick again.
- [Watchlist interactions](./issues/13-watchlist-interactions.md): one `userMovies` page prop, async optimistic router visits to idempotent set endpoints, and a partial reload. A Watchlist toggle everywhere; Mark as watched and Clear Watched on the detail page, card "⋯" menus and (Mark only) the picker. A toast with Undo for every action, with rollback and an error toast on failure. Undo restores a session-held snapshot of the latest action only if it still matches, and adds `user_movie_action_undone`. Guest actions survive sign-in as a single-use pending action in the session. Actions need `auth`, not `verified`.
- [Public pages: SSR, SEO metadata and sitemap scope](./issues/14-public-pages-seo.md): SSR on public pages only. Indexing behind a config flag, off until TMDB confirms the classification. `noindex` header on everything private. Titles, descriptions, OG backdrop and canonical slug-id URLs through one `<Head>` helper. `Movie` JSON-LD without `aggregateRating`. A daily sitemap index of all non-shell Movie rows. List links use canonical URLs once a row exists.
- [Reelio brand clearance](./issues/15-brand-clearance.md): not cleared. An iOS "Reelio: Movie & TV Watchlist" app built on TMDB already exists, and `reelio.com`/`.io`/`.co`/`.tv` are taken. **Decided:** drop the name. [Product name](./issues/20-product-name.md) picks and clears a replacement; "Reelio" stays the working name until then.
- [Verify TMDB behaviours with a live key](./issues/17-tmdb-live-checks.md): prefix search works but buries short queries (`inter` misses *Interstellar*), so search needs a results page. `append_to_response=watch/providers` gives a `watch/providers` key. `include_video_language` is honoured. Trending takes `page`, capped at 500. Upcoming with `region=US` returns `release_date` values outside its stated range. `free` and `ads` each appear for about 12% of US titles. Token-free fixtures are saved.
- [Deployment and operations](./issues/19-deployment-and-operations.md): Growth plan with scale-to-zero in production for now. Cloud managed queues (`default` and `imports`, Flex) replace the database queue, and Valkey replaces Upstash Redis. Deploys go through a deploy hook after CI, with backward-compatible migrations and rollback by redeploying a commit. `/up` for liveness and a new `/health` for readiness. Seven-day point-in-time recovery, a pre-launch restore drill, and restores re-delete accounts found in the logs. No external uptime monitor, so the 99.5% target is dropped for the prototype phase.
- [Product name](./issues/20-product-name.md): **Houselight** (house lights dimming as the film begins), chosen after three screening rounds. Reelio and SceneIt were rejected over existing movie products. Pending [Clear Houselight on the official registers](./issues/21-houselight-register-clearance.md); the rename is a find-and-replace implementation ticket.
- [Homepage curation](./issues/22-homepage-curation.md): a daily hero (Trending's top Movie with a backdrop), then Trending this week, Popular, Coming soon (US dates from `release_dates`, the next 60 days), Top rated, and three daily-rotated genre collections, 10 each. Deduplicated across sections in page order, no adult titles or poster-less Movies, the same for every visitor. Failed sections are hidden, with a full-page error only when everything fails.

## Out of scope

- Designing P1/P2 features (advanced filters, streaming-service preferences, personal ratings, viewing dates, custom lists, stats, export, Google sign-in, related movies, Rotten Tomatoes ratings via Fandango's licensing route). The spec lists them as follow-ups only.
- Building the MVP. That happens from the spec's tickets after this map is done.
- Metacritic ratings: dropped by the human; no free authorized source exists (see [OMDb ratings: coverage, terms and limits](./issues/03-omdb-ratings.md)).
- OMDb as a Rating source: not an authorized source for IMDb or Rotten Tomatoes scores ([same ticket](./issues/03-omdb-ratings.md)).
